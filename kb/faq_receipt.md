## Where do I get a receipt for accounting?

To find your receipts, log in to your CloudDesk account. Once you're in, go to the **Admin** section in the main menu. From there, select **Billing**. You will see a list of all your transactions, where you can view and download your receipts. If you need a specific receipt, you can search by date or amount. If you have any trouble finding what you need, feel free to reach out to our support team for assistance!
