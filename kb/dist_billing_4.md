# Managing Multiple Workspaces Billing

When managing multiple workspaces in CloudDesk, it's essential to keep track of billing for each workspace separately. Each workspace can have its own subscription plan, which allows for flexibility based on your team's needs. 

To manage billing effectively, regularly review your workspaces and their usage. This helps ensure that you're not overspending on unused resources. You can also consolidate billing information for easier tracking, allowing you to see all charges in one place.

Consider setting reminders for renewal dates or changes in your workspace plans. This proactive approach can help avoid unexpected charges and ensure that your workspaces remain active without interruption. 

For any billing inquiries or adjustments, reach out to CloudDesk support for assistance tailored to your specific situation.
