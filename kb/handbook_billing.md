# CloudDesk Billing Handbook

Welcome to the CloudDesk Billing Handbook. This document outlines essential information regarding billing processes, including invoices, payment methods, proration, dunning and past-due accounts, plan changes, and refunds. 

## Invoices

CloudDesk generates invoices at the beginning of each billing cycle. Invoices are sent to the registered email address associated with your account. Each invoice includes a detailed breakdown of the services provided, the billing period, and the total amount due. It's important to review your invoice for accuracy and to ensure that you are being charged correctly for the services you have used.

If you notice any discrepancies in your invoice, please contact our support team within 7 days of receiving the invoice. We will work with you to resolve any issues promptly.

## Payment Methods

CloudDesk accepts various payment methods to ensure a smooth billing experience. You can choose from the following options:

- Credit/Debit Cards: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover.
- PayPal: Link your PayPal account for quick and easy payments.
- Bank Transfers: For larger transactions, bank transfers can be arranged upon request.

To update your payment method, navigate to the billing section of your account settings. Be sure to keep your payment information current to avoid any interruptions in service.

## Proration

When you change your subscription plan during a billing cycle, CloudDesk automatically prorates your charges. This means you will only pay for the portion of the service you used on your previous plan before switching to the new one. 

For example, if you upgrade from a Basic plan to a Pro plan halfway through your billing cycle, you will be charged for the days you used the Basic plan and then charged the Pro plan rate for the remainder of the cycle. This ensures that you are only paying for what you use, as noted above.

## Dunning & Past-Due

If your payment fails or your account becomes past-due, CloudDesk will initiate a dunning process. This involves sending you a series of notifications to remind you of the outstanding balance. The timeline for these notifications is as follows:

1. **First Reminder:** Sent 3 days after the payment due date.
2. **Second Reminder:** Sent 7 days after the payment due date.
3. **Final Notice:** Sent 14 days after the payment due date, indicating that your account may be suspended if the balance remains unpaid.

To avoid service interruptions, please ensure that your payment method is valid and up to date. If you are experiencing financial difficulties, contact our support team to discuss potential solutions.

## Plan Changes

You can change your subscription plan at any time through your account settings. When you switch plans, the changes will take effect immediately, and your billing will be adjusted accordingly. 

As noted above, any changes made during a billing cycle will be prorated. This means you will receive a credit for the unused days of your previous plan and be charged for the new plan based on the remaining days in the cycle. 

Keep in mind that switching to a lower-tier plan may result in a reduction of features and services available to you.

## Refunds

CloudDesk has a clear refund policy to ensure customer satisfaction. If you are not satisfied with our services, you may request a refund within 14 days from the charge date. This standard refund window allows you to evaluate our services and determine if they meet your needs.

To request a refund, please contact our support team with your account details and the reason for your request. We will review your case and process the refund if it falls within the specified timeframe. 

Please note that refunds are not available for charges incurred after the 14-day window, as outlined above. Always review your billing statements carefully to ensure you are aware of your charges and any applicable refund policies. 

For further questions regarding billing, please reach out to our support team, and we will be happy to assist you.
