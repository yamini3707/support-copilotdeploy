# Troubleshooting: Account Locked After Failed Sign-In Attempts

If you find your CloudDesk account locked after multiple failed sign-in attempts, don't worry—this is a security feature designed to protect your account.

## Lockout Policy

- **Failed Attempts**: Accounts will lock after **10 failed sign-in attempts**.
- **Auto-Unlock**: The account will automatically unlock after **30 minutes**.
  
## Steps to Resolve

1. **Wait for Auto-Unlock**: If you can afford to wait, your account will be accessible again after 30 minutes.
  
2. **Contact Admin**: If you need immediate access, reach out to your system administrator. They have the ability to unlock your account sooner.

3. **Review Credentials**: Once unlocked, ensure you are entering the correct username and password to avoid future lockouts.

## Tips for Future Sign-Ins

- Use a password manager to keep track of your credentials.
- Enable two-factor authentication for added security.

For further assistance, consult the CloudDesk support team.
