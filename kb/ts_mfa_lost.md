# Lost MFA Device — Regaining Access

If you've lost access to your Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) device and need to regain access to CloudDesk, follow these steps:

### For Admins Resetting MFA

1. **Log in to Admin Panel**: Access your CloudDesk admin account.
2. **Navigate to Members**: Go to `Admin` → `Members`.
3. **Select the Member**: Find the member whose MFA needs resetting.
4. **Reset MFA**: Click on the option to reset their MFA. The member will receive instructions to set up a new MFA device.

### If the Only Admin is Locked Out

If you are the sole admin and cannot access your account due to a lost MFA device, follow these steps:

1. **Identity Verification**: Contact CloudDesk support to verify your identity. Be prepared to provide necessary information.
2. **Escalation to Account Recovery**: Once verified, your request will be escalated for account recovery. Follow the instructions provided by support to regain access.

Always ensure to set up backup recovery options for future incidents.
