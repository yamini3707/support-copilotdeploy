# Data Residency Overview

Data residency refers to the physical or geographical location where data is stored and processed. Organizations often have specific requirements regarding where their data can reside due to legal, regulatory, or compliance obligations. Understanding data residency is crucial for businesses that operate across multiple regions, as different jurisdictions may impose varying data protection laws.

CloudDesk prioritizes data residency by providing options for users to select data centers in specific regions. This ensures that organizations can maintain compliance with local regulations while optimizing performance. It’s essential to evaluate your data residency needs to ensure that your data management practices align with both legal requirements and organizational policies. By doing so, businesses can enhance data security, improve customer trust, and streamline operations across different markets.
