# Setting Spending Alerts

Setting spending alerts is a great way to keep track of your budget and manage your finances effectively. To begin, navigate to the settings section of your CloudDesk account. Look for the "Alerts" or "Notifications" tab. Here, you can customize your spending alerts based on your preferences.

You can choose to receive notifications via email or push notifications on your mobile device. Set thresholds for your spending, so you’re alerted when you approach or exceed your budget limits. This proactive approach helps you stay informed about your financial habits and encourages mindful spending.

Remember to review your alerts periodically to ensure they align with your current financial goals. By actively managing your spending through alerts, you can make more informed decisions and maintain better control over your finances.
