# How Session Timeouts Work

Session timeouts are an essential security feature designed to protect your account and data. When you log into CloudDesk, your session remains active for a set period of inactivity. If no actions are detected, the system automatically logs you out to prevent unauthorized access.

This timeout period helps safeguard sensitive information, especially in shared or public environments. You might notice a warning before the timeout occurs, giving you the option to extend your session if you’re still active. 

To avoid losing any unsaved work, it’s a good practice to save your progress frequently. If you do get logged out, simply log back in to continue your tasks. Understanding session timeouts can enhance your security and ensure a smoother experience while using CloudDesk.
