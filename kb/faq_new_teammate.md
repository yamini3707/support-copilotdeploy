### Adding Someone to Our Team

To add a new member to our team, an admin needs to follow these steps:

1. Log in to the CloudDesk account.
2. Click on the "Admin" section in the menu.
3. Select "Members" from the options.
4. Click on "Invite Member" or a similar button.
5. Enter the new member's email address and any other required information.
6. Send the invitation.

The new member will receive an email to join the team. Once they accept, they’ll be part of our CloudDesk team!
