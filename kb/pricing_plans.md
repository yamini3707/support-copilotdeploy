# CloudDesk Plans & Pricing

CloudDesk has four plans. Prices are per seat, per month, billed monthly or annually.

## Free — $0/seat
- Up to **3 seats**
- No API access
- No SSO
- No audit logs
- **30-day** data retention
- Community support only

## Pro — $12/seat
- Up to **25 seats**
- **Read-only API**, rate limited to **60 requests/minute**
- No SSO
- No audit logs
- **1-year** data retention
- Standard email support

## Business — $28/seat
- Up to **100 seats**
- **Full API** (read + write), rate limited to **600 requests/minute**
- **SSO (SAML / OIDC)** included
- No audit logs
- **3-year** data retention
- Priority support SLA

## Enterprise — custom pricing
- **Unlimited seats**
- **Full API**, rate limited to **6000 requests/minute**
- **SSO (SAML / OIDC)** included
- **Audit logs** included
- **Custom** data retention (default 10 years)
- Priority support SLA, dedicated CSM

## Feature availability at a glance

| Feature | Free | Pro | Business | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SSO | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Audit logs | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |
| API | ✗ | read-only | full | full |
| Priority SLA | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ | ✓ |

If a customer asks whether a feature is available on their plan, check their plan tier first
and answer from this table. Features not included on a plan require an upgrade — they are not
add-ons.
