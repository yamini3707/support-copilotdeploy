# Inviting External Guests

Inviting external guests to your CloudDesk workspace can enhance collaboration and bring fresh perspectives to your projects. To get started, ensure you have the necessary permissions to send invites. Navigate to the guest management section of your dashboard, where you can enter the email addresses of those you wish to invite.

When crafting your invitation, consider providing context about the purpose of their visit and any specific tasks or meetings they will be involved in. This helps guests prepare and ensures a productive experience. 

Once the invitations are sent, guests will receive an email with instructions on how to access your workspace. Remember to communicate any relevant guidelines or expectations to facilitate a smooth collaboration. Engaging external guests can lead to innovative ideas and strengthen partnerships.
