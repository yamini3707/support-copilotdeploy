# Trusting a Device for Faster Sign-In

Trusting a device can significantly enhance your sign-in experience with CloudDesk. When you choose to trust a device, it allows for quicker access without the need to re-enter your credentials every time you log in. This feature is particularly useful for frequently used personal devices, such as your laptop or smartphone.

To trust a device, simply follow the prompts during the sign-in process. After confirming your identity, you’ll have the option to mark the device as trusted. This means that future logins from that device will bypass certain security steps, streamlining your access.

However, remember to only trust devices that you own and use regularly. If you ever suspect that a trusted device may be compromised, it’s advisable to revoke its trusted status to protect your account.
