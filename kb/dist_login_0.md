# Password Best Practices

Creating a strong password is essential for protecting your accounts and personal information. Here are some best practices to follow:

1. **Use a Mix of Characters**: Combine uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special symbols to create a complex password.

2. **Length Matters**: Aim for a password that is at least 12 characters long. Longer passwords are generally more secure.

3. **Avoid Common Words**: Steer clear of easily guessable passwords, such as "password" or your name. 

4. **Unique Passwords for Each Account**: Don’t reuse passwords across different platforms. This limits the risk if one account is compromised.

5. **Enable Two-Factor Authentication**: Whenever possible, use two-factor authentication for an added layer of security.

6. **Update Regularly**: Change your passwords periodically to enhance security. 

By following these guidelines, you can significantly improve your online safety.
