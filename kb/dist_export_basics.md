# About Exporting Your Data

Exporting your projects and documents from CloudDesk is a straightforward process that allows you to back up your work or share it with others. You can export various file types, including PDFs, Word documents, and CSV files, depending on the nature of your project.

To export your data, navigate to the project or document you wish to export. Look for the "Export" option in the menu. Once selected, you will be prompted to choose your preferred file format. After making your selection, click on the "Export" button, and your file will be prepared for download.

Keep in mind that exporting large projects may take some time, and you will receive a notification once the process is complete. Always ensure that your data is saved and backed up regularly to prevent any loss. If you encounter any issues during the export process, consult the help section or reach out to support for assistance.
