# Signing in on Mobile

To sign in to CloudDesk on your mobile device, start by downloading the CloudDesk app from your device's app store. Once installed, open the app and locate the sign-in screen. Enter your registered email address and password. If you’ve enabled two-factor authentication, you’ll receive a verification code via your preferred method. Input this code to proceed.

If you encounter issues, ensure your internet connection is stable. You can also try resetting your password if you’ve forgotten it. For added security, consider using biometric options like fingerprint or facial recognition, if supported by your device. 

Should you continue to experience difficulties, consult the help section within the app or reach out to our support team for assistance. Enjoy seamless access to CloudDesk on the go!
