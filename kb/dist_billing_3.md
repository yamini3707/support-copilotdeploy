# How Usage is Metered

At CloudDesk, usage is metered based on various factors that reflect how you interact with our services. This includes the number of resources consumed, such as storage, processing power, and data transfer. Each component contributes to your overall usage metrics, which are tracked in real-time.

Monitoring your usage helps ensure optimal performance and allows you to make informed decisions about resource allocation. You can view your usage statistics through the CloudDesk dashboard, providing insights into your consumption patterns. 

Understanding how your usage is metered can help you optimize your workflows and manage costs effectively. Regularly reviewing your usage can also assist in identifying areas for improvement and potential savings. For detailed insights, refer to the usage reports available in your account.
