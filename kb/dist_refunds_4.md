# Switching between monthly and annual billing

Switching between monthly and annual billing on CloudDesk is a straightforward process designed to provide flexibility based on your needs. To change your billing cycle, simply navigate to your account settings. There, you'll find an option for billing preferences. 

When you switch to annual billing, you may benefit from cost savings compared to monthly payments. Conversely, if you prefer the flexibility of monthly billing, you can easily revert to that option. 

Keep in mind that any changes you make will take effect at the end of your current billing cycle. Always review your billing settings to ensure they align with your usage and budget. If you have any questions or need assistance, our support team is here to help!
