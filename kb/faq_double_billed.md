## FAQ: I Think You Billed Me Twice

If you believe you've been charged twice, please first check your billing statement for any charges. Look for the date and amount of each transaction. If you still think there’s an error, contact our support team. They will review your account and the charges to confirm if there was a mistake. Please note that we will only issue a refund after this verification process is complete. Thank you for your understanding!
