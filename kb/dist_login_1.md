# Clearing Browser Cookies for Sign-In Issues

If you're experiencing difficulties signing into CloudDesk, clearing your browser cookies may help resolve the issue. Cookies can store outdated information, leading to login problems.

To clear cookies, follow these general steps:

1. **Open your browser settings**: Look for the menu icon, usually in the top right corner.
2. **Find privacy or security settings**: This section typically contains options related to cookies and site data.
3. **Clear cookies**: Select the option to clear cookies. You may have the choice to delete cookies from specific sites or all sites.
4. **Restart your browser**: Close and reopen your browser to ensure the changes take effect.

After clearing cookies, try signing in to CloudDesk again. If problems persist, consider checking your internet connection or trying a different browser.
