# Data Retention — Free Plan

On the **Free** plan, deleted projects, docs, and activity data are recoverable for **30 days** from deletion. After 30 days the data is permanently purged and cannot be restored. To retain data longer, upgrade to a plan with a longer retention window or export before deleting.
