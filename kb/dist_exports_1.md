# Scheduling Recurring Exports

To streamline your workflow in CloudDesk, you can set up recurring exports for your data. This feature allows you to automate the process of exporting reports or datasets at regular intervals, ensuring you always have the latest information at your fingertips.

To schedule a recurring export, navigate to the export settings within your dashboard. Here, you can select the type of data you want to export and choose the frequency—daily, weekly, or monthly. Additionally, you can specify the format for your export, such as CSV or PDF.

Once configured, CloudDesk will automatically generate and send the exports to your designated email or storage location. This not only saves time but also minimizes the risk of missing important updates. Remember to review your scheduled exports periodically to ensure they meet your needs.
