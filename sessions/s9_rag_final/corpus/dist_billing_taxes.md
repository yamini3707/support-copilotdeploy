# Taxes and VAT on Your Invoices

When generating invoices through CloudDesk, it’s essential to understand how taxes and VAT are applied. Taxes are calculated based on the applicable rates for your region and the nature of the services provided. 

For businesses registered for VAT, it is crucial to ensure that your invoices include the correct VAT amount. CloudDesk allows you to configure tax settings to automatically apply the appropriate VAT rate to your line items. 

Each line item on your invoice will display the subtotal, tax amount, and total, clearly indicating the breakdown of costs. If your business operates in multiple regions, you can set different tax rates for each location to ensure compliance with local tax laws.

To manage your tax settings, navigate to the billing section in your CloudDesk account. Here, you can adjust tax rates and ensure that your invoices are accurate and compliant with regulations.
