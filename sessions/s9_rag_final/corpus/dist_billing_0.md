# How Seat-Based Pricing Works

Seat-based pricing is a flexible model designed to accommodate varying team sizes and needs. In this system, businesses pay for the number of active users or "seats" they require. Each seat typically represents an individual user who has access to the platform's features and resources.

This pricing model allows organizations to scale their usage according to their growth or project demands. For example, if a team expands, they can easily add more seats to ensure all members can collaborate effectively. Conversely, if the team size decreases, adjustments can be made to reflect the current needs.

Seat-based pricing promotes transparency and helps organizations manage their budgets more effectively, as costs are directly tied to user engagement. This way, companies can optimize their investment based on actual usage.
