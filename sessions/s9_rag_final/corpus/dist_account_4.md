# Deactivating a Member

To deactivate a member in CloudDesk, navigate to the 'Members' section of your dashboard. Locate the member you wish to deactivate by using the search function or scrolling through the list. Once you find the member, click on their profile to open detailed options.

In the profile view, look for the 'Deactivate' button. Clicking this will prompt a confirmation message to ensure that you want to proceed with the deactivation. Confirm your choice, and the member’s status will be updated accordingly.

Deactivating a member will restrict their access to the workspace, but their data and history will remain intact for future reference. If you need to reactivate the member later, you can easily do so by following a similar process. Always ensure to communicate with the member about any changes to their status.
