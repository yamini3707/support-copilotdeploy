# CloudDesk Refund Policy

## General refunds
CloudDesk offers a self-serve refund on any charge within 14 days of the invoice date. You can
request it from Billing → Invoices → Request refund, or by contacting support. Approved refunds are
returned to the original payment method and typically settle within 5–10 business days. A refund
does not cancel your subscription; your plan stays active until the end of the current period.

## Duplicate and incorrect charges
If you were billed twice for the same period, or charged after cancelling, the duplicate or
erroneous charge is always refundable regardless of the 14-day window. Flag the specific invoice and
support will reverse it.

## Monthly vs annual plans
For monthly plans, the 14-day window applies to each monthly charge. For annual plans paid upfront,
a refund within the first 14 days is prorated for any unused full months; after 14 days annual
prepayments are non-refundable but remain usable until the term ends.

## Enterprise agreements
Enterprise customers on a negotiated annual agreement are governed by the terms of their Master
Service Agreement (MSA), not by the self-serve policy above. Charges under an MSA are **not eligible
for self-serve refunds** and the 14-day window does not apply; any credit or refund must be arranged
through your assigned account manager under the agreed contract terms. This holds even if the charge
is within 14 days.

## Taxes and credits
Taxes already remitted to a tax authority are refunded only where the authority permits. Account
credits issued as goodwill are not cash-refundable and expire 12 months after issue.

## After a refund
Once a refund is issued you will receive an email confirmation with the reference number. If you
don't see it after 10 business days, contact support with that reference. Repeated refund requests
on the same subscription may be reviewed before approval.
