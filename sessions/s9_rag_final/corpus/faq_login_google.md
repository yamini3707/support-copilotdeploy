# Why can't I sign in with my company Google account?

If you're having trouble signing in with your company Google account, it might be because your organization is on a lower plan that doesn’t support this feature. Company-wide sign-in, which lets you log in using your Google or Microsoft account, is only available on our higher plans. 

This setup is managed by your company’s admin, so if you think you should have access, please reach out to them. They can check your plan and help set up the sign-in option if it’s available. If you have any other questions, feel free to contact our support team!
