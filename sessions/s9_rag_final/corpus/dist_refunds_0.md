# How Proration Works When You Upgrade

When you upgrade your CloudDesk plan, proration ensures that you only pay for the services you use. This means that the cost of your new plan is adjusted based on the time remaining in your current billing cycle. 

When you make the switch, the amount you've already paid for your current plan is credited towards your new plan. This way, you won’t lose any value from your previous payment. 

Proration helps create a smooth transition, allowing you to access enhanced features without worrying about overlapping charges. It’s designed to make upgrades straightforward and fair, ensuring you get the most out of your investment in CloudDesk. 

If you have questions about how proration applies to your specific situation, feel free to reach out to our support team.
