# Notification Preferences

Managing your notification preferences is essential for a streamlined experience with CloudDesk. Notifications help you stay informed about important updates, activities, and changes related to your account. 

To customize your notification settings, navigate to the settings menu within your account. Here, you can choose which types of notifications you wish to receive, such as alerts for new messages, system updates, or account activity. 

Consider opting for email notifications for critical updates while keeping less urgent alerts to in-app notifications. This way, you can prioritize what matters most to you without feeling overwhelmed. 

Regularly reviewing your preferences ensures you stay in control of your communication, allowing you to focus on what’s important. Adjust these settings anytime to adapt to your changing needs and ensure you never miss out on essential information.
