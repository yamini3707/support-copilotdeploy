### CloudDesk FAQ: The App is Really Slow Today

If you're experiencing slowness with CloudDesk, here are a few steps you can take:

1. **Check the Status Page**: Visit our [status page](#) to see if there are any ongoing incidents affecting performance.

2. **Refresh the App**: Sometimes, a simple refresh can resolve temporary issues. Try reloading the app to see if performance improves.

3. **Clear Cache**: If the problem persists, consider clearing your browser's cache or the app's data.

If you continue to experience issues, please reach out to our support team for further assistance.
