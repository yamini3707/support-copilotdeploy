# Understanding Account Credits

Account credits are a useful feature in CloudDesk that help you manage your subscription costs effectively. When you downgrade your plan, any unused portion of your previous subscription may be prorated and converted into account credits. This means that instead of losing value, you can apply these credits toward future charges.

Proration occurs when you switch plans mid-billing cycle, allowing you to receive a fair adjustment based on the time remaining on your previous plan. The credits you receive will automatically apply to your next billing cycle, reducing your overall payment.

It's important to keep track of your account credits, as they can significantly lower your costs when switching plans. You can view your available credits in your account settings, ensuring you stay informed about your balance and can make the most of your subscription choices.
