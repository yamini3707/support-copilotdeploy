# Data Retention — Business Plan

On the **Business** plan, deleted projects, docs, and activity data are recoverable for **1095 days** from deletion. After 1095 days the data is permanently purged and cannot be restored. To retain data longer, upgrade to a plan with a longer retention window or export before deleting.
