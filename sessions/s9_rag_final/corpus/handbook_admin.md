# CloudDesk Admin Guide

## Members & Seats

Managing members and seats is crucial for maintaining an organized workspace in CloudDesk. To add new members, navigate to the Admin panel and select the 'Members' section. Here, you can invite users by entering their email addresses. 

Each CloudDesk plan comes with a specific number of seats. Ensure that you monitor your seat allocation to avoid exceeding your plan limits. If you need to add more seats, consider upgrading your plan to accommodate your team's growth. 

To remove a member, simply select their profile and click 'Remove.' This action will free up a seat for future use. Regularly review your member list to ensure that only active users have access to your workspace.

## Roles & Permissions

Roles and permissions in CloudDesk help define what actions members can perform. The platform offers several predefined roles, including Admin, Editor, and Viewer. Each role comes with a specific set of permissions tailored to different responsibilities.

- **Admin**: Full access to all features, including member management and billing.
- **Editor**: Can create and modify content but cannot manage members.
- **Viewer**: Read-only access to content.

To assign roles, go to the 'Members' section, select a user, and choose the appropriate role from the dropdown menu. As covered above, managing roles effectively ensures that your workspace remains secure and organized.

## Single Sign-On

Single Sign-On (SSO) simplifies the login process for users by allowing them to access CloudDesk using their existing credentials from another identity provider. This feature enhances security and user experience. However, please note that SSO is available only with the Business plan or higher. 

To enable SSO, navigate to the 'Security' settings under the Admin panel. Follow the prompts to configure your identity provider and establish a secure connection. Once set up, users will be able to log in seamlessly without needing to remember additional passwords.

## Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) adds an extra layer of security to your CloudDesk account. By requiring users to verify their identity through a secondary method, such as a text message or authentication app, you can significantly reduce the risk of unauthorized access.

To enable MFA, go to the 'Security' settings in the Admin panel. You can choose the methods you want to support, such as SMS or authenticator apps. Encourage all members to activate MFA for their accounts to enhance overall security.

## Audit Logs

Audit logs are essential for tracking user activity within CloudDesk. They provide a comprehensive record of actions taken by members, which can be invaluable for security and compliance purposes. However, please note that audit logs are available only to Enterprise plan users.

You can access audit logs by navigating to Admin → Security. Here, you will find detailed information about user logins, changes made to settings, and other critical actions. Regularly reviewing these logs can help you identify any suspicious activity and maintain the integrity of your workspace, as covered above.

## Plan Changes

If your organization’s needs change, you may want to consider upgrading or downgrading your CloudDesk plan. To change your plan, go to the 'Billing' section in the Admin panel. Here, you can view your current plan details and explore available options.

When changing plans, be mindful of the features included in each tier. For example, features like SSO and audit logs are only available in higher-tier plans, as mentioned in the relevant sections above. Make sure to assess your team’s requirements before making a decision to ensure you select the most suitable plan for your organization.
