# CloudDesk Refund Policy

## Refund window

CloudDesk offers refunds on subscription charges within **14 days** of the charge date.
Requests made after the 14-day window has elapsed are **not eligible** for a refund, except
where required by local consumer law. The window is measured from the invoice date to the
date the refund is requested.

## What is refundable

- Duplicate charges (the same subscription charged more than once in a billing period) are
  **always refundable**, regardless of the 14-day window, once verified against invoice records.
- Charges made in error by CloudDesk (wrong seat count, wrong plan tier) are refundable once
  verified.
- A single most-recent subscription charge, if the customer cancels within the 14-day window.

## What is not refundable

- Charges older than 14 days (other than verified duplicates or billing errors).
- Usage-based overage fees that have already been consumed.
- Partial-month proration on downgrades — these are issued as **account credit**, not cash refunds.

## How refunds are processed

Support agents must **verify eligibility against the customer's invoices before promising a
refund**. Never confirm a refund based on the customer's description alone. Once verified,
refunds are issued to the original payment method and take 5–10 business days to appear.

Duplicate-charge refunds should reference the specific duplicate invoice ID. For any refund
above the standard monthly amount, or any request the agent cannot verify, escalate to the
billing team rather than approving directly.
