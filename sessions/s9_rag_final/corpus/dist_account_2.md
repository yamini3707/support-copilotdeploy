# Renaming Your Workspace

Renaming your workspace in CloudDesk is a straightforward process that allows you to better reflect your team's identity or project focus. To begin, navigate to your workspace settings by clicking on your profile icon in the top right corner. From the dropdown menu, select "Settings" and then choose "Workspace." Here, you will find the option to edit your workspace name.

Simply enter the new name you wish to use and save your changes. Keep in mind that renaming your workspace may affect how team members identify and access it, so it’s a good idea to communicate any changes to your team. This ensures everyone is on the same page and can easily find the workspace after the update. Enjoy your newly named workspace!
